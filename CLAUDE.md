# CLAUDE.md — Tik Tov: Israel Economic Geography (3D)

This project features:
- A 3D stylized map of Israel (Haifa & North, Tel Aviv & Gush Dan, Jerusalem, Be'er Sheva & Negev, Mediterranean coastline, Route 6, and Highway 1).
- An evolving 3D **Tik** (backpack / portfolio) worn on the player's back (and strapped to the car roof rack while driving) with 4 color-coded asset pockets.
- Unzip-your-Tik inventory UI (`P` or `Tab`), Decision Journal, and 100-point anti-gambling rubric.
